How these are measured

Pulls to kill: every Mythic pull on the boss up to and including the first kill. Pulls under 10 seconds are resets and are ignored, and the same pull logged twice counts once. When the logs don't have the kill, or have under 75% of Raider.IO's count (missing nights), Raider.IO's pull count is used instead.

Raid nights to kill: raid nights with at least one pull on the boss, up to the kill. A new night starts after a gap of more than 6 hours. Logs only.

Time on boss: total combat time across those pulls. Logs only.

Item level on kill: the raid's average equipped item level on the kill pull, shown as a difference from 1-200. Logs only.

Fight left: how much of the whole fight was left when a pull ended, from Warcraft Logs' fight percentage. Pulls loaded before 23 Oct 2026 estimate it from the stage reached and boss health, giving each stage an equal share, because boss health resets between stages on several bosses.

Pulls to a sub-10% wipe and close-out pulls: the first pull that ended with under 10% of the fight left (or the kill, if that came first), and the pulls from there to the kill. Together they split slow learning from slow finishing.

Longest plateau: the most pulls in a row that didn't beat the guild's best pull by at least 1 point of fight left.

Late wipes: the share of progression wipes that ended in the stage the guild killed the boss in. On bosses that cycle back to stage one, or have no stages in the logs, it is wipes with 30% or less of the fight left. Shown as a difference in points from 1-200.

Pulls per hour: pulls on the boss divided by raid time on it, counted from the first to the last pull of the boss on each night.

Item level on first pull and gained: the raid's average item level on its first counted pull of the boss, and how much it rose by the kill.

Hours and nights per week: raid nights with a progression pull on any boss (pulls up to each boss's first kill), from the first to the last pull of the night, averaged over the weeks with logs. A guild counts only when its logs cover at least 75% of the weeks between its first and last Raider.IO kill. Only Mythic boss pulls are logged, so trash and Heroic time is left out.

Second read: deaths, roster and comp come from a second read of each log, best-ranked guilds first, which takes a few days to cover the tier. Until all of a guild's pulls on a boss have had it, the guild is left out of these numbers.

Deaths per pull, early deaths and avoidable deaths: player deaths across the progression pulls divided by the pulls. Early deaths are in the first 60 seconds of a pull. Avoidable deaths are to the one ability per boss picked by hand as avoidable; bosses without one show a dash.

Fast collapses: the share of wipes that ended within 90 seconds of the raid's third death, shown as a difference in points from 1-200.

Players used and roster: different players in the progression pulls on the boss. For the whole tier, regulars are players in at least 3 progression pulls, the core is the guild's 20 most-used players, and a pull has the core when 16 of them are in it. New regulars per week counts regulars first seen after the guild's first two logged weeks.

Raid DPS, healer HPS and comp match: from Warcraft Logs rankings of the guild's first kill: everyone's DPS added up, the healers' HPS added up, and how close the spec mix is to 1-200's average mix (cosine similarity, 100% is the same).

Brackets: Raider.IO Mythic world rank for the tier under All, and rank within the region under US or EU, each down to 5000. 1-200 is the Hall of Fame and the baseline for multiples; 1-20 and 21-200 split it. Brackets with no guilds in a region are hidden. Each figure is the bracket median and needs at least 10 guilds. Guilds with fewer than a quarter of the bracket median's pulls are left out as incomplete logs.

As of: the numbers as they stood at the end of that week of the tier. A kill's numbers never change, so a past week counts only the kills made by then. Ranks are today's.

Sources: Raider.IO for ranks, kill dates and its pull counts; Warcraft Logs for pulls, nights, time, fight progress, stages, item level, deaths, roster and rankings.
